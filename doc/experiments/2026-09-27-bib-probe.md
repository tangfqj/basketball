# Milestone 2, step 0 — are bib numbers legible?

**Verdict: go.** Bib numbers are large and readable in most crops, on the front *and* the back of the bib.

## Setup

`scripts/probe_bibs.py <video> --frames N`: N frames spread evenly between game start and end; every
dataset player box cropped at native 4K (top 60% of the box = head to hips); contact sheets in
`data/_bibs/<video>/` (git-ignored), labeled with team and bib number from the dataset.

Looked at IMG_0104 (purple vs green, 10 frames, 60 crops) and IMG_0107 (yellow vs purple, 6 frames,
36 crops).

## Findings

| | |
|---|---|
| Crop size (upper body, 4K) | median ~120 × 160 px (range 66–258 × 117–258) |
| Digit height | roughly 50–80 px: far above what a small classifier needs |
| Front / back | numbers printed on **both** sides; shooters facing the basket show their back number |
| Legible by eye | ~78% of crops in IMG_0104, ~65% in IMG_0107 (dark digits on yellow have lower contrast) |
| Unreadable cases | side view, arm or another player in front, motion blur, and a few dataset boxes that are off the person |

Every player's number was legible in several of the sampled frames, so a **per-track** vote should
be reliable even though single frames are not.

## Bib numbers across the 12 Outdoor videos (from the dataset)

- Only 9 distinct numbers occur: **1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10, 11, 12**. All numbers in the test videos
  also occur in the dev videos, so a closed-set classifier (9 numbers + "unreadable") covers them.
- **The same number can appear on both teams** in one video (IMG_0107: two #10; IMG_0108: #6 and #9
  twice). Identity must be *team + number*, as planned (PL-1).
- **6 and 9 often play together** (e.g. IMG_0104 team B). The classifier must not be made
  rotation-invariant; upright crops keep 6 and 9 apart.

## Risks for the next steps

- **Label noise:** some dataset boxes lag the player or cover two people, and crops often contain part
  of another player's bib. Training crops should be filtered (box overlap with other players) and
  the per-track vote must tolerate a few wrong readings.
- **Contrast:** yellow bibs with dark digits are harder than purple / green; check per-colour
  accuracy.

## Decision

Skip the off-the-shelf OCR baseline: the number set is tiny and closed, and labeled crops are free,
so a trained classifier is the direct route. OCR can still be tried later if the classifier
underperforms.
